Time assumptions in this build
Goal: 7–8 hours. For calculation, Loochori uses the midpoint: 7.5 hours.

You: off 9a · 8a shift 7a · 9a shift 8a · late shift 9:30a.

Cori: off 9a · Wed/Thu ~9:15a · Fri 6a · Sat ~6:45a.

Your commute: bus ~25m to work (usual range 20–30m) · ~35m home (usual range 30–40m).

Cori commute: ~5m walk each way.

These are planning estimates, not tracking. The app uses the midpoint/typical value to calculate the day.
